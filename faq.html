Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the X2 Smartcard Allinone software, hardware and support resources.

What is X2 Smartcard Allinone?

It is the official 2026 EMV chip formatting and smartcard management suite for authorised professional, laboratory and educational use.

Which companion tools are included with the additional software package?

The companion package covers ATR Tool, JCOP English, JCOP Manager, MSRX, CardPeek and related IST files used alongside the main X2 application.

What hardware is required?

A typical laboratory setup uses an Omnikey smart card reader, a Deftun / MSRX magnetic reader-writer, and unfused JCOP blank cards.

How are purchases completed?

Product pages list current packages. Payment pages accept Bitcoin or Solana. After sending payment, submit the email form so access or shipping details can be arranged.

How should the software be used?

Use is restricted to authorised professional and educational environments and must follow the laws of the country in which it is operated.

How can I get in touch?

Use the Contact page to send a message. The form is monitored and responses are provided when appropriate.
